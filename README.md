# GoiMon

## Overview
GoiMon is a management app for small restaurants/food shops, helping manage menu items, staff, and revenue. The app lets customers choose dishes in-store and allows staff to place orders for customers (depending on how the shop operates).

## Users
- Owner/Manager: track menu items, staff, and revenue.
- Staff: take and place orders for customers.
- Customers: view and choose dishes in the shop.

## Key features
- Menu management (add/edit/delete items, update prices).
- Staff management (track list and information).
- Revenue management (daily/monthly summaries).
- In-store ordering: customers self-order or staff order on their behalf.

## Basic flow
1. Owner updates the menu.
2. Staff/customers choose items and create an order.
3. The system records the order and updates revenue.

## Technology (current)
- Backend: ASP.NET (Minimal API).
- OpenAPI available in development.
